## About the Portfolio

The portfolio includes selected academic, professional, and collaborative projects completed through the University of Maryland, Extern, Phoenix Color, Break Through Tech, and nonprofit partnerships.

Each case study explains:

- The problem or research question
- The methods and tools used

## Featured Case Studies

### TrueBridge Public Health Analysis

Analyzed more than 10,000 public-health records using Python, pandas, and Google Colab. The project examined relationships between lead service-line risk and community health indicators and translated the findings into practical recommendations.

### SAYA Compensation Benchmarking

Completed an independent nonprofit compensation analysis through Extern in partnership with PwC and South Asian Youth Action. I compared compensation across more than 20 nonprofit organizations and developed recommendations related to salary competitiveness and employee progression.

### Phoenix Color Continuous Improvement

Observed manufacturing workflows, interviewed employees, investigated process gaps, and standardized more than 30 operating procedures. I also created a reusable SOP template and visualized the movement of materials through the manufacturing process.

### D.C. Lead Water Crisis

Conducted a multi-year research project examining the 2004 Washington, D.C. lead water crisis. The project explored the relationships among infrastructure, government agencies, regulations, testing methods, scientific evidence, and resident experiences.

### Post-COVID Housing Market Analysis

Used Tableau and Zillow Home Value Index data to compare changes in home values across metropolitan areas. The project focused on regional affordability and the housing decisions facing students and early-career professionals.

### Polling Place Accessibility

Worked with a University of Maryland team and the Southern Poverty Law Center to investigate polling-place accessibility in Mobile County, Alabama. We used ArcGIS, Census data, and network analysis to estimate driving times and evaluate whether travel created unequal burdens for Black and Latine communities.

### ShellWell

Collaborated with a four-person development team through Break Through Tech to build an accessibility-centered routine tracker. The application was created with Flask, Python, HTML, and CSS to support students who benefit from additional structure and task organization.
